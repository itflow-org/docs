The search box in the top bar searches all of ITFlow at once. Results show as you type, grouped by what they are, and you can open one without your hands leaving the keyboard.

## Search from any page

1. Click **Search everywhere** in the top bar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) on any page.
2. Start typing. After two letters, results start to show.
3. Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through them and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open one. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it in a new browser tab. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the search.

![The search window with results for "ravenwood" grouped into clients, projects, contacts and domains](gs-search.webp "Results are grouped by type: here a client, its onboarding project, its contacts and its domains")

With nothing typed, the search window lists the clients you opened most recently, so getting back to one is two key presses.

## What it finds

Search looks through clients, tickets, projects, contacts, locations, assets, networks and IP addresses, licenses, services, credentials, domains, certificates, documents, files, invoices, quotes, expenses, income, vendors, recurring tickets, products and tags. It only shows what your role lets you open.

A few things that are handy to know:

- **Numbers come first.** Type a ticket, invoice or quote number - `1810`, or `TCK-1810` - and that record is the first result, so <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it straight away.
- **Phone numbers, IP addresses and MAC addresses match.** Type the number a client is calling from to find the contact, or an IP address to find the device that has it and the network it belongs to.
- **Tags match.** Searching `VIP` finds the clients, assets and tickets tagged VIP, with the tag next to each, and a Tags group to open each tag's list.
- **Products match by part number.**

![Searching for a number brings up the ticket with that number first](gs-search-number.webp "A ticket number comes first, ready to open with Enter")

## Search everything

The last line in the search window is always **Search everything**. It opens the full search page, which also looks inside ticket conversations and the text of documents - things the quick search leaves out to stay fast.

The full page shows how many of each kind it found along the top. Click one to jump to it, or **Open in Tickets** (and the like) to see the matches in their own list, where you can filter and sort them.

![The full search page for "backup", with counts for each kind of record](gs-search-page.webp "Search everything: every match, including ticket conversations and document text")

## Searching a list

Every list in ITFlow also has its own search box at the top, which searches just that list - and keeps any filters you have set. See [Lists, quick looks and bulk actions](/getting-started/lists).

> [!TIP]
> Admins can also find a setting this way: type `late fee`, `logo` or `smtp` in the search window and pick the setting to go straight to it.
