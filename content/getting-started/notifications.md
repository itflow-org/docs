ITFlow lets you know when something happens that you'll want to act on. The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications you haven't read yet, and the count updates every minute without reloading the page.

## Open your notifications

Click the **bell**. A panel opens with your notifications grouped by day, newest first.

![The notifications panel with Unread and All tabs](gs-notifications.webp "The panel under the bell: unread first, with the client each one is about")

- **Unread** shows the ones you haven't dealt with. **All** shows the read ones too.
- Click a notification to open what it's about - the ticket, the invoice, the shared link - and mark it read.
- **Mark all as read** clears them all at once.
- **View all notifications** opens the full Notifications page.

## The Notifications page

The full page lists every notification you've had, with a search box, a filter by type and **Unread** or **All**. Point at a notification to mark it read or unread on its own.

![The Notifications page with search, a type filter and Unread and All buttons](gs-notifications-page.webp "The Notifications page: search, filter by type, and mark one or all as read")

## What you're notified about

What reaches you depends on your role and what you're working on. Common ones are:

- **Tickets** - a ticket assigned to you, a client replying to one of your tickets, a task waiting for your approval, and a client's rating of a ticket
- **Recurring tickets** and **projects** assigned to you
- **Shared links** - a client opening a link you shared (you can switch this off for a link when you make it), a link locking after wrong tries, and a login a client sent back to you
- **Billing** - an invoice paid online, and stock running low on a product
- **Renewals** - domains, certificates and warranties about to expire, UPS batteries due for replacing, and service reviews due
- **The client portal** - a client adding a contact, giving someone portal access or adding a document
- **For admins** - repeated failed sign-ins, emails that couldn't be sent, backups and updates

> [!TIP]
> Some of these come by email too, such as tickets assigned to you and SLA warnings. Which emails go out, and to which addresses, is set by your admin in **Admin > Settings > Notifications**.
