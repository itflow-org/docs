ITFlow lets you know when something happens that you'll want to act on. The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications you haven't read yet, and the count updates every minute without reloading the page.

## Open your notifications

Click the **bell**. A panel opens with your notifications grouped by day, newest first.

![The notifications panel with Unread and All tabs](gs-notifications.webp "The panel under the bell: unread first, with the client each one is about, and the desktop notifications switch at the bottom")

- **Unread** shows the ones you haven't dealt with. **All** shows the read ones too.
- Click a notification to open what it's about - the ticket, the invoice, the shared link - and mark it read.
- **Mark All as Read** clears them all at once.
- **View all notifications** opens the full Notifications page.
- **Desktop notifications** has a switch that shows whether they're on in this browser - see below.

## Desktop notifications {#desktop}

ITFlow can also tell you on your computer's desktop, like an email or chat app does, when:

- a ticket is assigned to you, or
- a client replies on one of your tickets.

Click the **bell**, then turn on the **Desktop notifications** switch at the bottom of the panel, and allow notifications when your browser asks. They only show while you're away from ITFlow - in another program or another browser tab. Click one to open the ticket.

The switch is for the browser you're using, so turn it on in each browser you work in. Under it, the panel says whether they're **On in this browser** or **Off in this browser** - turn the switch off to stop them. If it says they're **Blocked by this browser**, the switch is greyed out: allow notifications for your ITFlow site in your browser's settings, then turn them on again.

## Your open tickets on the browser tab {#tab-count}

The [tickets list](/service-desk/tickets#views)'s browser tab starts with how many open tickets are assigned to you - **(3)**, say - the same number as **Mine**. It's kept up to date in the background, so you can see it from another tab. Other pages keep their own title.

## The Notifications page

The full page lists every notification you've had, with a search box, a filter by type and **Unread** or **All**. Point at a notification to mark it read or unread on its own.

![The Notifications page with search, a type filter and Unread and All buttons](gs-notifications-page.webp "The Notifications page: search, filter by type, and mark one or all as read")

## What you're notified about

What reaches you depends on your role and what you're working on. Common ones are:

- **Tickets** - a ticket assigned to you, someone [mentioning you](/service-desk/working-a-ticket#mention) in an internal note, a client replying to one of your tickets, a task waiting for your approval, and a client's rating of a ticket
- **Reminders** - an event on the calendar, and work booked with you, at the time [set for it](/service-desk/calendar#reminders)
- **Recurring tickets** and **projects** assigned to you
- **Shared links** - a client opening a link you shared (you can switch this off for a link when you make it), a link locking after wrong tries, and a login a client sent back to you
- **Billing** - an invoice paid online, draft invoices waiting to be sent (and how many have been left more than a week), a sent quote running out in three days with no answer, and stock running low on a product
- **Renewals** - domains, certificates and warranties about to expire, UPS batteries due for replacing, and service reviews due
- **The client portal** - a client adding a contact, giving someone portal access or adding a document
- **For admins** - repeated failed sign-ins, emails that couldn't be sent, backups and updates

> [!TIP]
> Some of these come by email too, such as tickets assigned to you, mentions and SLA warnings. Which of these go out - including the bell notice and the email for a ticket assigned to you - is set by your admin in [Admin > Settings > Notifications](/admin/mail#notifications).
