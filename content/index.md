## New to ITFlow?

Start with [Finding your way around](/getting-started/tour). It covers the menus, the dashboard and how ITFlow is laid out around your clients. Then read [Lists, quick looks and bulk actions](/getting-started/lists): every list in ITFlow works the same way, so once you know one, you know them all.

Installing ITFlow on a server of your own? Start with [Before you install](/install/requirements). If you are setting ITFlow up for your team, go to [Administration](/admin/settings). It covers adding your team, mail, billing settings and the templates that save everyone time.

## About these docs

These pages describe ITFlow 26.11. The screenshots come from a demo company, Summit IT Partners, and its made-up clients. Your ITFlow shows your own clients, and only the parts your role lets you open, so a menu item or button in a screenshot may not be there for you. If something is missing, ask whoever runs ITFlow at your company.

Click any screenshot to see it full size. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) on any page to search the docs.
