A license is software or a subscription the client has: Microsoft 365 Business Premium, endpoint protection, their dental practice software. It tracks how many seats were bought and who uses them, the keys, the vendor, and when it renews.

## The licenses list

![A client's licenses with seat bars and renewal dates](doc-licenses.webp "Licenses: seats in use against bought, and when each renews")

Each row has a seat bar - red when more seats are in use than were bought - and a renewal date that turns amber and then red as it gets close. When there's something to see, tabs show **Renewing soon**, **Expired**, **Over seats** and **No seats assigned**.

Tick licenses and use **Bulk Action** to **Renew** several at once, tag them or archive them.

## Add a license

1. Open the client, go to **Licenses** and click **New License**. To start from one your admin set up, pick it under **Start from a template** (or **New from Template** in the button's menu) - see [License and vendor templates](/admin/license-and-vendor-templates).
2. On **Details**, type the **Name** and **Version**, pick its **Type** (Software as a Service, Desktop Application, Security Software and so on) and **Vendor**, and add a description.
3. On **License**, set the **License type** (Subscription, Device, User, Site, Concurrent, Perpetual and so on), the **Seats bought**, the **License key** - or **Individual keys**, one per line, to hand out one at a time - the **Order / invoice reference**, and when it was **Purchased** and when it **Renews / expires**.
4. On **Seats**, pick the **Devices** and **Users** that have it.
5. On **Notes**, add anything else - how it's licensed, where it's managed.
6. Click **Create**.

## The license's page

![A Microsoft 365 license with its seats, keys and vendor](doc-license.webp "A license's page: seats, the key, the vendor's support details, and who has it")

- **Renew** moves the renewal date on, and **New** has a **Renewal Ticket** - a ticket for the renewal, already filled in - as well as keys, documents and files.
- **License** shows the seats in use against bought, the type, the key, when it renews, when it was bought and the order reference - each copyable.
- **Vendor** shows your account number, PIN, and the vendor's support line, email, site, hours and SLA.
- The tabs list the **Seats** - the devices and users that have it, with **Manage Seats** - the **Keys** and who each is handed to, linked **Credentials**, **Documents**, **Files** and **History**.

### Individual keys

Some software has a key per install. Paste them on the license, one per line, and hand each to a device or a user from the **Keys** tab. When a device or user comes off the license, their key goes back to the pool for the next one. An asset's and a contact's pages show their licenses with the key to install.

## Renew a license

Click **Renew** on the license (or tick several in the list and use **Bulk Action > Renew**). Move the renewal date on by a term - a month, a year - or to a date you pick. The window shows each new date before you save, and records the new order reference.

> [!TIP]
> Licenses renewing soon show on the dashboard, the client's overview, the calendar (**Renewals**) and the **Renewals & Expirations** report. Clients see theirs in the portal too, without the keys.

## Billing for seats

When you resell a license, its seats can be billed on the client's recurring invoice and follow the seat count each month. See [Recurring invoices](/billing/recurring-invoices#bill-per-user-per-device-or-per-seat).
