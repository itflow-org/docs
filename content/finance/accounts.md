Accounts are where your money is: your checking and savings accounts, the company credit card, petty cash, and your payment processor, where card payments wait to be paid out to the bank. Every payment, expense and transfer goes into or out of one, so each account's balance follows the real one.

![The accounts list](fin-accounts.webp "Accounts: what each holds, what's owed on the cards, and this month in and out")

Along the top: what's **In the accounts** (cards aside), what's **Owed on cards**, and what came **In** and went **Out** this month - money moved between your own accounts left out.

Each account shows its **Kind**, what it **Holds** (what's owed, for a card), this month in and out, its **Latest** line, and how far it's been reconciled. Click one to open its transactions. Its **⋮** menu has **Transactions**, **Income into it**, **Expenses from it**, **Reconcile**, **Edit**, **Transfer from it** and, for a card, **Pay the Card**.

## Add an account

1. Click **New Account**.
2. Give it a **Name** - "Operating Checking", "Business Visa", "Stripe".
3. Pick its **Kind**: **Checking**, **Savings**, **Credit card**, **Cash**, **Payment processor** or **Other**, and its **Currency**.
4. Type the **Opening balance** - what it held (or, for a card, owed) on the day you start using ITFlow for it.
5. Say **What it is for** and add **Notes** - the bank, the last four digits, who can sign.
6. Click **Create**.

Archive an account you've closed, once it's empty, from its **⋮** menu. It's kept on the **Archived** tab, with its history.

## Transactions

An account's **Transactions** is its ledger: every payment, other income, expense, transfer and reimbursement on it, in date order, with the balance after each.

![An account's transactions](fin-transactions.webp "Transactions: the account's ledger, with the balance after every line")

Along the top: what it **Holds now**, and the money in, out and the difference for the dates you pick. The tabs are **All**, **Money in**, **Money out**, **Transfers** and **Uncleared** - lines not on a reconciled statement yet. A line that's cleared has a tick by its date.

Pick another account at the top, or every account at once. Narrow by date, category or client; **More filters** narrows by kind, how it was paid and the amount. Click a line for its quick look.

## Move money between accounts

A transfer moves money from one of your accounts to another: paying off the card, putting the quarter's tax aside in savings, a payout from your payment processor to the bank.

1. Click **New Transfer** - on **Transactions**, or in the menu beside **New Account**.
2. Pick the account it came **From** and the one it went **To**. **Swap** turns them round.
3. Type the **Amount** and **Date**, and how it was **Moved by**.
4. Add **Notes** - what it was for - and click **Transfer**.

![The New Transfer window](fin-transfer-new.webp "New Transfer: from one of your accounts to another")

- **Paying the card**: transfer from the bank to the card account, or use **Pay the Card** in the card's menu. What's owed on the card goes down.
- **Banking checks and cash**: transfer from your cash account to the bank, and tick the checks and cash in the deposit under **In this deposit**. The amount and notes fill in, and they won't be offered again.

A transfer isn't income or spending, so it's left out of your income, expenses and reports.
