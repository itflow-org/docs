**Income** lists every bit of money that comes in:

- **payments** on invoices - recorded from the invoice, paid online, or imported from a bank statement
- **other income** that isn't for an invoice - interest, a partner rebate, a referral fee, a deposit

![The income list](fin-income.webp "Income: this month and this year, what recurring invoices bring in, and what's still owed")

Along the top: what came in **This month** against the same days last month, the year **So far** with its monthly average, what your **Recurring** invoices bill a month, and what's **Still owed** on invoices.

The tabs are **All**, **Invoice payments**, **Other income**, **Paid online** and **No category**. Search, and narrow by date, category, client, account and payment method. Tick lines and use **Bulk** to set their category, account, payment method or client, or delete them.

Click a line for its quick look. A payment's shows the invoice it paid, with **Open Invoice** and **Email Receipt** - see [Taking payments](/billing/payments#change-or-remove-a-payment).

## Record other income

For money that isn't for an invoice. A payment on an invoice is added from the invoice instead, so the invoice is marked paid.

1. Click **New Income**.
2. Pick the **Category** - Interest, Rebates, Referral Fees - and the **Amount**.
3. Pick the account it was **Paid into** and the **Date**.
4. If it came from a client, pick them under **From client**. It then counts for them in Income by Client and Client Profitability.
5. Pick how it was **Paid by**, add a **Reference** such as the deposit number, and a **Description**. Its first line shows in the list.
6. Click **Add Income**.

![The New Income window](fin-income-new.webp "New Income: for money in that isn't a payment on an invoice")

To bring in a bank statement's deposits all at once, use **Import Deposits** in the menu beside **New Income** - see [Importing statements](/finance/importing-statements#import-deposits).

> [!TIP]
> **Income Summary** and **Income by Client**, in the same menu, are the reports for what came in by category and month, and what each client paid against the period before.
