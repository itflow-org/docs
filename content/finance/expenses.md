An expense is money the business spent: a bill from a vendor, a card purchase, hardware for a client, a team lunch. Recording them keeps your accounts right, gives your accountant the receipts, and lets you bill a client for what you bought on their behalf.

You don't have to type every one. [Recurring expenses](/finance/recurring-expenses) record the regular ones for you, and [Import Statement](/finance/importing-statements) brings in a whole card or bank statement at once.

## The expenses list

![The expenses list](fin-expenses.webp "Expenses: what you've spent this month and this year, and what's missing a receipt")

Along the top: what you've spent **This month** against the same days last month, the year **So far**, what your recurring expenses come to a month, and how many expenses from the last 90 days have **No receipt**.

The tabs are **All**, **For a client**, **No receipt**, **To bill** (billable, not on an invoice yet), **Billed** and **To reimburse** (paid personally by someone on the team). Search, and narrow by vendor, category, client, account, how it was paid and date. The total of what's shown is under the list.

Tick expenses and use **Bulk** to set their category, account, payment method or client, make them billable, reimburse them, or delete them.

## Add an expense

1. Click **New Expense**.
2. Pick the **Vendor** - who it was paid to - and type the **Amount**. Picking a vendor fills in the rest from your last expense with them.
3. Pick the **Category** and the **Date**.
4. If the amount includes tax, pick the **Tax included** rate. The **Tax** part is worked out for you - change it to what the receipt says.
5. Pick the account it was **Paid from** and how it was **Paid by**. If someone on the team paid it out of their own pocket, pick **Paid personally** and who paid it.
6. If it was for a client, pick the **Client**. It counts as a direct cost of theirs in the Client Profitability report. Switch on **Bill it on to them** to bill it, with a markup.
7. Add a **Reference** (their invoice or order number) and a **Description**.
8. Attach the **Receipt** - a photo or a PDF. On a phone, you can take the picture right there.
9. Click **Add Expense**.

![The New Expense window](fin-expense-new.webp "New Expense: who, how much, what for, where it was paid from, and the receipt")

The vendors here are your own - the businesses you buy from - not your clients' vendors.

## The expense's quick look

Click an expense to see it with its receipt.

![An expense with its receipt](fin-expense-ql.webp "An expense's quick look: the receipt, the vendor, the client it was for, and what it bills")

It shows the vendor, category, account, reference and client. For a billable expense, it says what it bills and whether it's waiting in [Unbilled Time](/billing/unbilled-time) or already on an invoice. **History** lists every change.

Along the bottom: **Receipt** to download it (or **Attach** one if there's none), **Edit**, **Copy** and **Delete**.

## Bill an expense to the client

When you buy something for a client - a part, a license, a courier - switch on **Bill it on to them** and set the markup. Your admin sets the markup it starts with. The expense waits under **To bill** and in the client's [Unbilled Time](/billing/unbilled-time#one-client), and goes on their next invoice at its cost plus markup, with a line of its own. You can change the price before billing it.

To make several billable at once, tick them and use **Bulk > Set Billable**.

## Pay someone back

An expense **Paid personally** is owed to whoever paid it, and waits under **To reimburse**. Tick the ones to pay back and use **Bulk > Reimburse**: pick the account it's **Paid back from**, the date, how and a reference, and click **Pay Back**. Each expense still counts as spent on its own date; the money leaves the account on the day you pay it back.

Mileage works the same way - see [Trips and mileage](/billing/trips#pay-drivers-back).

> [!TIP]
> **Expenses Report**, in the menu beside **New Expense**, shows spending by category and month. **Spend by Vendor** in [Reports](/finance/reports) shows who you pay the most - and the vendors to send 1099 forms to.
