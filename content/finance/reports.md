**Reports** gathers every report you can open, in groups, with a line on what each shows. Type in **Find a report** to jump to one.

![The reports page](fin-reports.webp "Reports: every report in its group, with what each one shows")

Every report works the same way: pick a period, narrow it to one client where that makes sense, then **Print** it or **Export** it to CSV. Each shows only what your role can see.

## Finance

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Profit & Loss** | Income against spending by month or quarter, with the margin and last year beside it |
| **Income** | Money received by category and month - invoice payments and other income |
| **Expenses** | Spending by category and month |
| **Budget vs Actual** | Plan what you'll spend each month by category, and see how the real spending compares |
| **Spend by Vendor** | Who you pay the most - and the vendors paid 600 or more, for 1099 forms |
| **Tax Collected** | Tax on sales by rate, invoiced or paid, with taxable, exempt and non-taxable sales and the tax paid on purchases |
| **Mileage** | Distance driven by agent and client, and what it comes to at your mileage rate |

![The Profit & Loss report](fin-report-pl.webp "Profit & Loss: income, expenses and profit by month, with last year beside it")

## Sales and billing

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Receivables Aging** | What each client owes, split by how late it is |
| **Income by Client** | What each client paid in the period, against the period before |
| **Client Profitability** | Each client's income against the hours spent on them - your effective hourly rate |
| **Recurring Revenue** | Monthly and yearly recurring revenue by client and category |
| **Quotes Pipeline** | Open quotes, your win rate, and the value won and lost |
| **Inventory** | Stock on the shelf and what it's worth, what came in and went out, and what to reorder |
| **Unbilled Work** | Billable tickets that are done but not invoiced yet, with the time and value on them |
| **Time Detail by Client** | Every reply with time on it, by client and ticket, rounded to your billing increment |

## Service desk

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Ticket Overview** | Tickets opened and resolved, how fast, where they came from and when |
| **Open Ticket Aging** | Every open ticket by age, agent and client - the backlog, oldest first |
| **Tickets by Client** | Ticket volume, time worked and response times for each client |
| **Technician Performance** | Hours logged, billable share, utilization and tickets for each agent |
| **SLA Performance** | Response and resolution targets met, by month, priority and agent |
| **SLA by Client** | Response and resolution targets met for each client |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | How clients rated their closed tickets, by agent and client |
| **Ticket Templates** | How often each template is used, and the ones that aren't |
| **Canned Responses** | How often each canned response is used, by whom, and the ones that aren't |

## Clients and documentation

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Client Review** | One client's quarter on a page - tickets, SLAs, assets, renewals and what to do next. Made to print for a business review. |
| **Renewals & Expirations** | Domains, certificates, licenses and warranties coming up for renewal, or already lapsed |
| **Asset Lifecycle** | How old the hardware is, its warranty and operating system support, and what's due for replacement |
| **Documentation Coverage** | What's documented for each client, and what's missing |
| **Document Templates**, **License Templates**, **Vendor Templates** | How much each template is used, and which clients that should have one don't |
| **Credential Rotation** | Passwords that haven't changed in a while, oldest first |

## Activity

**Audit Activity** shows what was done in ITFlow - changes, deletions, passwords looked at, exports and sign-ins - over time, out of hours, and by person, client and kind of record.

> [!TIP]
> **Client Review** is also on each client's **⋮** menu. Print it before a quarterly business review to walk the client through their quarter.
