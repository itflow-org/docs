A recurring expense is something you're committed to paying again and again: the office rent, colocation, your partner licensing, insurance, a lease. ITFlow records the expense for you each time it falls due, so your spending is always up to date without anyone typing it.

![The recurring expenses list](fin-recurring-expenses.webp "Recurring expenses: what you're committed to, what it comes to a month, and when each is next recorded")

Along the top: what they come to **A month** (quarterly ones count as a third, yearly as a twelfth), what's recorded in the **Next 30 days**, how many are **Running**, and how many **Need a look**.

The tabs are **Active**, **Next 7 days**, **Late**, **Paused**, **Ended** and **All**.

## Set one up

1. Go to **Finance > Recurring Expenses** and click **New Recurring Expense**.
2. Pick the **Vendor** and the **Amount each time**, and the **Tax included** if any.
3. Pick **How often** - monthly, quarterly or yearly - and when the **First one** is. Leave **Ends** empty unless it stops on a date, such as a lease or financing that's paid off. The schedule is written out in words under it.
4. Pick the **Category**, and the account it's **Paid from** and how it's **Paid by**.
5. If it's for one client, pick the **Client** and how it's billed:
   - **Our cost - not billed on** - you pay it, and it counts against the client in Client Profitability
   - **Billed on to them each time** - each expense waits in Unbilled Time to go on their invoice
   - **Covered by their recurring invoice** - pick the recurring invoice that pays for it, so you can see the margin it leaves
6. Add a **Reference**, such as the account or contract number - it goes on each expense - and a **Description**.
7. Click **Set Up**.

![The New Recurring Expense window](fin-recurring-expense-new.webp "New Recurring Expense: who, how much, how often, and whether a client pays for it")

## Run, pause and change

Click one for its quick look: what it records, when, and the expenses it has recorded so far.

- **Record Now** records the next one straight away.
- **Pause** stops it until you **Resume** it.
- **Edit** changes it, from the next one on.

If one's next date has passed without it being recorded, it's listed under **Late** and its quick look says when it was due. **Record Now** records it, or **Move On** skips to the next date without recording it.

> [!TIP]
> Link the licensing, hosting and backup you resell to the client's recurring invoice - with **Covered by their recurring invoice**, or **Add** on the invoice's **Costs** card. The recurring invoice then shows the margin it really leaves each month. See [Recurring invoices](/billing/recurring-invoices#the-recurring-invoices-page).
