A canned response is a reply your team sends again and again - "Account unlocked", "Following up - no reply yet", "Escalated to the vendor" - written once, and put into a ticket in one go. Agents use them from the reply box; see [Canned responses](/service-desk/canned-responses).

![Canned responses](adm-canned.webp "Canned responses: what each says, where it's offered, what it sets up and how often it's used")

Each row shows what the response says, the ticket categories it's **Offered on**, what it sets up on the reply - who sees it, the status after it, time worked - and how often it's been used.

The tabs are **Active** and **Archived** for the shared ones, and **Agents' own** - the ones agents made for themselves. One of those can be shared with everyone from its menu.

## Write a response

Click **New Canned Response**. The window has three tabs.

![Editing a canned response](adm-canned-edit.webp "A canned response: its text with placeholders, where it's offered, and what it sets up")

**Response**:

1. Give it a **Name**.
2. Pick the ticket categories it's **Offered on** - "Account and Access" for "Account unlocked". Leave it empty to offer it on every ticket.
3. Write the **Response**. Click a placeholder to put it where the cursor is: `[Contact]`, `[First Name]`, `[Client]`, `[Ticket]`, `[Subject]`, `[Ticket Link]`, `[Asset]`, `[Site]`, `[Project]`, `[Agent]`, `[Date]`, `[Month]`, `[Quarter]` and `[Year]` are filled in from the ticket as it goes in.

Anything else in square brackets - `[Date of visit]` - stays as it is, and the agent is asked to fill it in before sending.

**Sets up** - what the reply becomes: an internal note, public note or email reply, the ticket's status after it - such as **Resolved** or **On Hold** - and the time worked to add.

**Files** - attachments that go with it, such as a how-to PDF.

## Share and keep them tidy

- **Copy** a response to start a similar one.
- **Archive** one you no longer want offered.
- **Import** and **Export** move them to and from a file.
- **Starter canned responses** adds a library of common ones - see [Maintenance](/admin/maintenance#starter-content).
- **How they are used** shows which responses go into replies, on how many tickets and by whom - and the ones nobody uses.
