Most MSPs sell the same licenses, and deal with the same vendors, client after client. Templates set them up once, so each client's copy starts complete and consistent.

## License templates

A license template is a license you sell or look after again and again: Microsoft 365 Business Premium, an endpoint protection agent, a backup product.

![License templates](adm-license-templates.webp "License templates: each license, what it starts with, and how often it's used")

Open one to see **What a license starts with**: its name, type and license type, how often it renews, its vendor, the product it's **Sold as** from your catalog, and notes for your team - where it's managed, how renewals work.

![A license template](adm-license-template.webp "A license template: what each client's license starts with, and who has one")

- **New license for a client** - or **For several clients** - makes licenses from it in one go.
- **Who should have it** - every client, or the clients with a tag. The **Required** tab then shows who's missing it.
- **Clients with it** lists every license made from it, with their seats.

Agents pick a template when they add a license to a client - see [Licenses](/documentation/licenses). A license sold as a catalog product can be billed per seat on a recurring invoice - see [Recurring invoices](/billing/recurring-invoices#bill-per-user-per-device-or-per-seat).

## Vendor templates

A vendor template is a vendor your clients use again and again - the ISPs, the hardware makers, the distributors, line-of-business software - with its website, support line, hours, SLA and what their support asks for.

![Vendor templates](adm-vendor-templates.webp "Vendor templates: vendors your clients share, set up once")

- **Add to clients** adds it as a vendor to several clients at once.
- **Who should have it** - every client, or those with a tag. **Required** shows who's missing it.
- **Out of step** shows clients' vendors made from it whose details now differ from the template - a support number that changed, say - so you can bring them back in line.

Agents pick a template when they add a vendor to a client - see [Vendors](/clients/vendors). A client's own account number, PIN and contacts stay on the client's vendor.

## Starter libraries

Both have a starter library - **Starter license templates** and **Starter vendor templates** in the menu beside **New** - with the licenses and vendors most MSPs use. Add them, then change or archive what you don't need. See [Maintenance](/admin/maintenance#starter-content).

**Import** and **Export** move templates to and from a file, to share them between installs.
