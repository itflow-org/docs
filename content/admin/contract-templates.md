A contract template is one of your standard agreements: "Fully Managed Services", "Co-Managed IT", "Block Hours". **New Contract** can [start from one](/billing/contracts#add), which fills in the contract's type, term, renewal, notice, cover and terms.

![Contract templates](adm-contract-templates.webp "Contract templates: each with its term, what it covers, and how many contracts were started from it")

**Admin > Contract Templates** shows each template with its type, its term and renewal, the notice, its cover - support hours, rate and net terms, and the SLA for each priority - and how many contracts were started from it. There are tabs for the ones **In use** and the **Archived** ones. Search, and sort by name, term or contracts.

## Add or change a template

Click **New Template**, or **Edit** on a template's menu. The window has three tabs:

- **Template** - its **Name**, a **Description**, the **Type**, **Term**, whether it **Renews itself for another term**, and the **Notice to end it**
- **Cover** - the support hours, hours included each month, rates, net terms and an SLA for each priority, as on a [contract](/billing/contracts#add)
- **Terms** - the agreement's words, with the same [placeholders](/billing/contract-signing#placeholders) as a contract

A template has everything a contract has except the client and the dates.

## Copy, archive or delete

- **Copy** makes "Name (copy)", to start a variation.
- **Archive** takes a template off New Contract and keeps it. **Restore** brings it back.
- **Delete** leaves the contracts started from it as they are.
