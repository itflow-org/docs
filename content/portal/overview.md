The client portal is your clients' side of ITFlow. A contact you give access to signs in with their email address and can:

- raise tickets, follow them and reply, and ask for things with your request forms
- see and pay their invoices, accept quotes and save a card for autopay
- look up their devices, licenses, domains, documents and colleagues

It carries your company's name and works on a phone as well as a computer. What each contact sees depends on their roles - see [Giving contacts access](/portal/access).

## Signing in

Clients sign in at the same address as you, with their email and password, or with their Microsoft account if you've set that up. **Forgot password?** emails them a link to choose a new one. When you [let people sign up](/portal/invites#sign-up), **Create an account** is there too.

## Home

![The portal's home page](portal-home.webp "Home: what needs them, their open tickets, what they can ask for, and their devices")

Home puts what matters first:

- Along the top: their **Open tickets**, how many are **Waiting on you** (on them), what's **Due** and when, and **Renewals coming up**. **Pay invoices** is a click away when something is owed.
- **Needs you** lists what's waiting on them: tickets you've replied to, tickets you've resolved for them to confirm, approvals, overdue invoices and quotes waiting for an answer.
- **Open tickets**, **Your devices** and what's **Coming up** for renewal.
- **Ask for something** shows your request forms - "Ask for a new computer", "Someone is leaving" - see [Tickets and requests](/portal/tickets).
- **Need us fast?** gives your phone number and email.

Announcements you post - planned maintenance, a holiday closure - show at the top of every client's home, or one client's. Your admin adds them in **Admin > Settings > Client portal**.

## Finding their way around

The bar along the top has **Home**, **Tickets**, **Billing** (invoices, quotes, recurring invoices, payments, statement, and cards and autopay) and **Your IT** (devices, licenses, domains and certificates, documents and contacts). **New ticket** is always there.

Their name opens **Your account** - their details, the emails they get from you, their password, two-step sign-in and phone PIN - and **Activity**, every sign-in and everything they did in the portal. They can switch to dark mode there too.

![The portal on a phone](portal-home-phone.webp "On a phone, the portal fits the screen")

## See it as they do

On a contact's page in ITFlow, **View portal as them** opens the portal as that contact sees it, in a new tab. It's read only - nothing you click changes anything or is recorded - so it's the quickest way to answer "I can't see my invoice".
