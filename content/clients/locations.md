A location is one of the client's sites: the head office, a branch, a warehouse. Its page is what a technician needs on the way out of the door - the address with directions, the opening hours, who to ask for when they arrive, access notes, and everything that's there.

## The locations list

![A client's locations list](cl-locations.webp "Locations: the primary location first, with directions, phone and hours, who to ask for and what's on site")

The primary location comes first. Each row shows the address with **Directions**, the phone number and opening hours, who to **ask for** on site, and how many people, devices, networks and racks are there.

The **⋮** on a row has **Quick look**, **Edit** and **Make primary**.

## Add a location

1. Open the client, go to **Locations** and click **New Location**.
2. On **Details**, give it a **Name**, and a description, tags and a photo of the building if you like. Switch on **Primary location** if this is now their main site.
3. On **Address**, type the street address. **Directions** and the map use it.
4. On **Contact**, pick the **On-site contact** to ask for, and the site's phone, fax and **Hours**.
5. On **Access**, add the **Access notes**: door codes, where to park, where the equipment is.
6. Click **Create**.

A client's first location becomes its primary location by itself. To make another one primary, use **Make primary** in its menu.

## The location's page

![The Main Office location page](cl-location.webp "A location's page: getting there, who to ask for, access notes, and everything at the site")

Along the top:

- **Directions** opens the address in your maps app, and **Call** rings the site.
- **New** starts something already set to this location: a **Ticket here**, a **Visit here** on the calendar, a contact, asset, network, rack, document or file upload, or a **Trip here**.
- **Move here** moves contacts, assets, networks or racks to this location in one go - when a client moves office, say - or links a document or file to it.
- **Edit**, and **⋮** for the rest.

Down the side:

- **Getting there** - the address, phone and hours.
- **Ask for** - the person to ask for on site, with their phone and email.
- **Access notes** - door codes, where the comms cupboard is, where to park. Type in it and it saves when you click away.

The tabs list everything at the location: **People**, **Assets**, **Networks**, **Racks**, **Tickets**, **Trips**, **Documents**, **Files** and **History**.

> [!TIP]
> Tickets show the site's address, hours and access notes in their **Site** card, so whoever goes out has them without looking them up. See [Working a ticket](/service-desk/working-a-ticket).
