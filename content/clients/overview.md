Opening a client takes you to its **Overview**. It's built to answer "what do I need to know about this client right now?" before you pick up the phone or start on a ticket.

![The client overview: the header, Needs attention, and the Technical section](cl-overview.webp "The top of the overview: the client's header, what needs attention, and their technical side")

## The header

Along the top: the client's initials, name, industry and tags, their primary contact with phone and email, their primary location, and details such as their website, how long they've been a client and who referred them.

**New Ticket** raises a ticket for this client. The **⋮** menu has **New Project**, **Edit Client**, **Export Data**, **Client Review** and **Archive Client**.

## Needs attention

A row of tiles counting what needs doing for this client. Each one opens a list of exactly those. Only the ones that apply show up, such as:

- tickets **past their SLA**, **unassigned tickets** and **overdue projects**
- work **resolved, not invoiced** and **expenses to bill**
- **service reviews due**, **passwords due for a change**, **licenses over seats** and **certificate problems**
- documents, licenses or vendors the client should have and doesn't
- warranties, licenses, domains and certificates **expired** or **expiring soon**
- [contracts](/billing/contracts) **ending** or **renewing soon**

## Technical

- **Open tickets** - the client's open tickets, with how many were opened and resolved in the last 30 days.
- **Quick notes** - anything the next person should know: gate codes, quirks, who to ask for. Type in it and it saves when you click away.
- **Key contacts** - the client's contacts with a role (primary, important, billing or technical), primary first, with their phone numbers and email.
- **Favorites** - the assets and logins you've starred for this client, with a login's password and 2FA code a click away.
- **Coming up** - the next 14 days: scheduled tickets, events and recurring work.
- **Projects** and **Services**, with what needs a look.
- **Renewals** - domains, certificates, licenses and warranties expired or due in the next 45 days.
- **Assets** - how many of each kind, and how many warranties are covered or expired.
- **Shared links** that are still open, and **Recent activity** - the latest changes, with a link to the client's audit log.

## Financial

If your role covers billing, the overview ends with the client's money:

![The Financial section: balance, paid this year, recurring, not yet invoiced and terms](cl-overview-financial.webp "Financial: what they owe, what they've paid, what's waiting to be billed")

- **Balance** - what they owe, and how much of it is overdue.
- **Paid this year**, against last year.
- **Recurring** - what their recurring invoices bring in a month.
- **Not yet invoiced** - billable tickets and expenses waiting to be billed, and what they come to. Click it to open [Unbilled time](/billing/unbilled-time) for this client.
- **Contract** - the [contract](/billing/contracts) in force, and the prepaid hours left across their contracts.
- **Terms** - their hourly rate, payment terms, tax ID and tax rate, or that they're tax exempt. While a contract is in force, its rate and net terms show instead.
- **Unpaid invoices**, **Recurring invoices** and a chart of what was **invoiced and paid** each month for the last year.

> [!TIP]
> Before a call or a quarterly review, **Briefing** on the overview has AI sum the client up - how things stand, what keeps coming up, renewals and what to raise with them. It's there when your admin has set up AI. See [Using AI](/getting-started/ai).
