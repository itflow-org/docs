A banner is a note your team sees along the top of their pages - "On credit hold, no work without approval", "Call before going onsite", an outage everyone should know about. Clients never see banners. For a note on the client portal instead, see [announcements](/admin/client-portal#announcements).

A banner is for one client or for every client:

- **One client** - along the top of the client's pages, and of its tickets, invoices, quotes, recurring invoices and projects, however they're opened. A ticket opened from the main tickets list still shows it.
- **Every client** - along the top of every page.

![A ticket with an Urgent banner for its client, a Heads up banner, and a Notice for every client](cl-banners.webp "Urgent goes first, then the client's own, then the ones for every client")

## How loud it is {#tone}

Each banner is shown as one of three:

- **Notice** - blue
- **Heads up** - yellow
- **Urgent** - big and red, always first

Anyone can hide a **Notice** or **Heads up** with its **×**. It stays hidden for them until someone changes the banner, then it comes back. Nobody can hide an **Urgent** banner - it shows on every page it's for until it ends or is archived.

## Add a banner for a client {#client}

1. Open the client and click **⋮ > New Banner**.
2. Give it a **Title** and, if it needs one, a **Message** - who to ask, what to check. Web addresses become links.
3. Pick how it's **Shown**: **Notice**, **Heads up** or **Urgent**.
4. Set when to **Show from** and **Until**. Empty, it shows straight away and until you archive it.
5. Click **Post Banner**.

![A new banner for a client](cl-banner-new.webp "New Banner from the client's menu - it's for that client")

Anyone whose role can change clients can add a banner for a client they have access to.

## Change, archive or delete one {#change}

The client's **⋮** menu lists its banners under **New Banner** - the ones showing now, and any still to come, marked **(scheduled)**. Click one to change it. At the bottom of the window:

- **Archive** - nobody sees it, and it moves to **Archived** in Admin > Agent Banners, where **Restore** brings it back
- **Delete** - it goes for good

A banner that has ended stops showing by itself.

## Every client's banners {#admin}

**Admin > Agent Banners** lists every banner - for every client and for each client - with how loud it is, whether it's **Showing**, **Scheduled** or **Ended**, who it's for, and when. Search by title or message, or pick a client, or **Every client**, to see just theirs. **Archived** has the ones that are over.

![Admin > Agent Banners](adm-agent-banners.webp "Every banner, for every client and for one, with where each stands")

Only admins can add a banner for every client. Click **New Banner** and leave **For** on **Every client**, or pick a client. The **⋮** on each row has **Edit**, **Archive** or **Restore**, and **Delete**.

> [!TIP]
> Using a tag to warn your team about a client? A banner can't be missed - make it **Urgent** and it's at the top of every page for that client, tickets included.
