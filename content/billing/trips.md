A trip is a drive: to a client's site for a ticket, to the supplier to collect parts, to the bank. Logging trips does three jobs at once:

- **billing** - trips for a client can go on their invoice, at your trip charge
- **paying agents back** - trips in an agent's own vehicle add up to what they're owed, at your mileage rate
- **a mileage log** - every trip, who drove, where and why, for your records and your accountant

## Log a trip

The quickest way is from the ticket: its **⋮** menu has **Log a trip**, and so does its **Trips** card. A client's location has **Log Trip**. Or go to **Finance > Trips** and click **New Trip**.

1. Check the **Date** and the **Driver**. You log your own trips; agents with Financial access can log them for anyone.
2. Pick the **Client** and **Their site** - or **Our own - not for a client** for your own errands. Pick **For the ticket** to link it to a ticket.
3. Type where you went **From** and **To**. Picking a site fills in where to, and picking a ticket fills in what it was for.
4. Type the **Miles one way**, and leave **Round trip** on for there and back. ITFlow shows the whole trip. Or open **Odometer readings** and type the readings **At the start** and **At the end**.
5. Leave **In their own vehicle** on if the driver used their own car, so they're paid back for it.
6. **Bill the client for it** starts on for a billable ticket's trip. It says what the trip will bill.
7. Say **What for**, and click **Log Trip**.

## The trips list

![The trips list](bill-trips.webp "Trips: the miles this month and this year, what's owed to drivers, and every trip")

Along the top: the miles **This month** against the same days of last month, the year **So far**, **Yours this month**, and what's owed **To pay back** to drivers.

The tabs are **All**, **Mine**, **For a client**, **For a ticket**, **Our own**, **To pay back** and **To bill**. Icons in each row show a round trip, the driver's own vehicle and whether it's billed.

Click a trip for its quick look: where it went, the client, site and ticket, what it's paid back and billed, and **Directions** to see the route on a map. **Return Trip** logs the way back, and **Copy** logs the same trip again.

![A trip's quick look](bill-trip-ql.webp "A trip: the route, who drove, what it's paid back and what it bills")

Tick trips and use **Bulk** to set the driver, client, vehicle or whether they're billable.

## Bill trips to the client

Billable trips wait in [Unbilled Time](/billing/unbilled-time) with the client's tickets and expenses, and go on their invoice as a line each - named "Travel" unless your admin changed it, with the route, date and distance in its description. You can change a trip's price there before billing it.

What a trip bills is set by your admin: a **Trip charge** for each trip, a rate **Billed a mile**, and how many miles the charge covers before the rate starts.

## Pay drivers back

Trips in an agent's own vehicle add up under **To pay back**, at your mileage rate.

![Trips to pay back](bill-trips-topay.webp "To pay back: who's owed what for driving their own car")

1. Open the **To pay back** tab and click **Pay Mileage** beside the driver.
2. Untick any trips to leave for later. Check the **Rate** - the usual rate, or the one for when these were driven - and the **Date** of the mileage expense.
3. Switch on **Pay it back now** if you're paying them today, and pick the account it's **Paid back from**, how, and a reference.
4. Click **Pay Mileage**.

![The Pay Mileage window](bill-pay-mileage.webp "Pay Mileage: the trips, the rate, and paying the driver back now or later")

The trips become a mileage expense the agent paid personally, under the client each was for, so each client's costs include the driving. If you didn't pay it back straight away, pay it later from the expense with **Reimburse** - see [Expenses](/finance/expenses).

> [!TIP]
> **Mileage Report**, in the menu beside **New Trip**, totals the miles for a period by driver and by client, with what they come to at a rate you can change - your tax-deduction rate, say. **Mileage Rates** takes your admin to where the rates are set, in **Admin > Settings > Invoices and billing**.
